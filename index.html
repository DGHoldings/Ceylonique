Our story

Healthier choices, without giving up taste

Ceylonique Icon was founded by Anuradha Liyanage, who holds a degree in Food Science. She saw a growing demand for high-quality, protein-rich foods people could trust, and set out to make premium peanut butter that brings together nutrition, quality ingredients and real flavour.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How does checkout work?

Add jars to your cart, enter your name, phone number and address, then tap "Send order on WhatsApp". WhatsApp opens with your full order already written. Press send and we'll reply to confirm.

How do I pay?

We confirm your total and payment method with you on WhatsApp before we dispatch. You can tell us your preference at checkout.

How much is delivery and where do you deliver?

Delivery charges depend on your location. We confirm the delivery fee and timing with you on WhatsApp when we receive your order.

Do I need WhatsApp to order?

WhatsApp is the quickest way. If you can't use it, email us at ceyloniqueicon@gmail.com with your order and we'll take it from there.

How should I store my jar?

Keep it closed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, and always use a clean, dry spoon.
